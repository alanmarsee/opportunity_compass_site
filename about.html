About the project

A student-centered compass for finding your next opportunity.

Built as an interdisciplinary learning project serving Cyber Policy and Ethics and Global Studies at the Walker Institute, Opportunity Compass connects global perspectives with thoughtful approaches to technology, policy, and human judgment.

The platform was designed with students at the center—recognizing that the gap between knowing an opportunity exists and actually pursuing it is often a matter of clarity, not ambition. By surfacing relevant internships, research programs, and campus resources in one place, Opportunity Compass helps students move from uncertainty to a concrete first step.

2 core pathways Internships and research are the two primary opportunities the Compass helps students discover and pursue.
Human-guided Every recommendation is reviewable by advisors and faculty.

Why this exists

Good opportunities are only useful when students can find them.

Students often encounter opportunities across many offices, websites, and informal networks. This project creates a clear front door—without pretending that one tool can replace mentorship, advising, or student agency.

▷

Our mission & goals

Make internships and research opportunities easier for students to discover, understand, and pursue.

♙

Human-in-the-loop oversight

Technology can organize possibilities, but people provide context. Guidance remains supported by educators and advisors.

⊗

Transparency & data privacy

We collect only what is needed to support exploration. Students can understand how their responses are used.
